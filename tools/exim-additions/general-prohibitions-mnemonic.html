Memory aid

Two mnemonics for the 10 General Prohibitions

1. Learn the order with one sentence. Each word starts with the same letter as the key word of one prohibition, from 1 to 10.

Can Dogs Dig Deep? Every Elephant Probably Tramples Very Knowingly.

#WordProhibition
1CanControlled items to listed countries
2DogsDe minimis US content
3DigDirect product of US technology
4DeepDenial Order
5EveryEnd use or end user
6ElephantEmbargoed destinations
7ProbablyProliferation of weapons
8TramplesTransit through prohibited countries
9VeryViolating licence terms or orders
10KnowinglyKnowledge of a violation

The three D’s are easy to keep apart. Prohibitions 2 and 3 are about the product, and 4 is about the buyer.

2. Learn the logic with “What, Who, How, Know”. Ask these four questions in order:

  1. What is it, and how American is it? (1–3)
  2. Who gets it, for what use, and where? (4–6)
  3. How is it used or routed? (7–8)
  4. What do I know, and am I following the rules? (9–10)
