## Gaps

- **Lecturer name and exam details.** No deck names a lecturer, and no file gives the exam type, length, date, mark split or a kisi-kisi. `notebook.json` leaves out `lecturer` and `exam`, no `exam-questions.md` was written, and the mock exam's 120 minutes and 100 marks are an assumption modelled on the FreshMart case's 30/40/30 point format.
- **Readings named on reference slides but not in the folder:** "The Global Entrepreneur" (HBR, 2008); "15 Steps Entrepreneurs Must Take Before Going Global" (Forbes, 2022); "How to Identify Business Opportunities" (HBS Online); "Systematic Review on Business Model of Cultural-Based Product Business" (IEEE, cited in Weeks 5 and 6); "Protecting your assets abroad" (Boyletts Law, Week 7). Only their titles are used. The pack does not cover their contents.
- **International content is thin in places.** The Week 1, 6 and 7 deck titles say "international", "across cultures" and "abroad", but the slides mostly teach general entrepreneurship and United States law. International points in the pack come from the slides that exist (PCT, Berne Convention, foreign trademark registration, translation testing, offshore outsourcing) and from the cases. Nothing was added from outside sources.
- **Image-only slides.** Some figures are images (Figures 1.1, 3.4, 5.2 and Table 4.2). Figure 5.2, the customer segmentation matrix for a diabetes device, cannot be read at slide resolution, so the pack teaches the matrix idea without its cell contents. Figures 1.1 and 3.4 are the same image (the entrepreneurial process grid).
- **"Textbook" slides** (slide 2 of each deck) show only a cover image. The textbook is identified from the reference slides as *Launching New Ventures*. No edition is stated, though the 2018 copyright example on Week 7 slide 23 and the "previous edition (2016)" on Week 1 slide 16 suggest a 2018 or later edition.

## Conflicts

- **Industry life cycle stages.** Week 1 slide 9 says industries are "born, grow, decline and die". Week 4 slide 11 says "birth, maturity, and ultimately decline". Figure 4.1 shows six stages (emerging, growth, differentiation, shakeout, maturity, decline). The pack teaches the six-stage figure as the fullest version and mentions the Week 1 wording.
- **"Resource Sources" heading (Week 5 slide 16).** The heading says resource sources, but the content is revenue streams and revenue models. The pack treats it as revenue models and notes the heading in `w5-revenue-costs`.
- **Copyright rights and the First Sale Doctrine (Week 7 slide 20).** The slide credits the six rights to "the First Sale Doctrine [Section 106]". In the Copyright Act, Section 106 grants the rights and the first-sale doctrine (Section 109) limits them. The pack teaches the list as given and adds an Update note.
- **Copyright term wording (Week 7 slide 21).** The slide says "life of the copyright holder plus 70 years". The statute measures from the author's life. The pack keeps the course figure and adds an Update note.
- **EuroDisney debt figure.** The case text gives debts "estimated at FF421bn", which cannot be right next to starting capital of FFr3bn and total cost of about $4.4bn (it is probably a misprint for about FFr21bn). The class analysis repeats FFr421bn and later writes FFr442bn. The pack leaves the debt figure out and relies on cash reserves (FFr1.1bn) and cost exhibits instead.
- **EuroDisney analysis arithmetic.** The class analysis calls attendance "10% short" (10 million against 11 million is about 9% short) and the French visitor gap "31%" (50% forecast against 29% actual is 21 percentage points). The pack uses the case's raw figures.
- **EuroDisney hotel figures.** The case text says "close to 5,200 rooms" and 18,200 rooms required by 2017. Exhibit 7 totals 5,184 and Exhibit 4 shows 18,000. These differences are minor and the pack uses Exhibit 7.
- **LocalBite blueprint channel payouts.** For a Rp 33,000 pack, the blueprint says direct orders return Rp 26,897 "with no deductions", which is less than the price. It also gives Rp 26,400 for a 20% commission app. These figures do not reconcile. The pack shows only the 20% commission calculation.
- **LocalBite scope.** The case describes modern Indonesian snacks sold online in general. The blueprint narrows this to frozen cireng in Bandung with chili levels. The pack presents the blueprint as one possible extension, not as the case facts.
- **Spelling.** The slides use American spelling and the FreshMart analysis uses British spelling. The pack follows the slides.
