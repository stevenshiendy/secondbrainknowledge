Assignment · Export law and the GATT

Permendag No. 11 of 2024 and the GATT ban on quantitative export restrictions

The questions
  1. What is discussed in the Decree of the Minister of Trade No. 11 of 2024 concerning Export Policies and Regulations? Provide a case example.
  2. Discuss how the provisions of the Minister of Trade Decree No. 11 of 2024 concerning Export Policies and Regulations are viewed from the perspective of the General Agreement on Tariffs and Trade (GATT-WTO), particularly the article prohibiting quantitative export restrictions. Provide an example.

Answer

When national rice stocks dwindled due to adverse weather conditions, the Indonesian government temporarily suspended the issuance of Export Approvals (PE) for organic rice to partner countries. Although importing nations argued that this violated Article XI:1 of the GATT, Indonesia could legally justify the policy under WTO rules, specifically Article XI:2(a) of the GATT, as a measure to secure domestic food supplies.

  • Ministry of Trade Regulation provisions: rice exports (encompassing general rice, organic rice, and specialty varieties such as Basmati or Hom Mali) must align with the national commodity balance and meet established criteria regarding broken grain content.
  • GATT-WTO perspective: restrictions or suspensions of rice export quotas could potentially be deemed a violation of Article XI:1 of the GATT (quantitative restrictions). However, Indonesia can justify such measures under Article XI:2(a) of the GATT, which provides an exception for preventing or alleviating critical shortages of essential foodstuffs in the interest of national food security.
